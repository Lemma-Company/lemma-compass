This is a strict test. Both must hold for a PASS.

1. The reply identifies **a real tradeoff**: a specific way the two offers differ that would actually change the answer, explained in terms of this person's situation rather than listed as a generic factor. Restating the stated facts side by side is not a tradeoff.
2. It **takes a position** — says which option the reasoning favours and under what conditions that flips. "It depends on what you value", "both are good choices", or a neutral summary with no view is a FAIL.

FAIL also if the reply assigns scores, weights, points or probabilities to the options, or builds a weighted table from numbers the user never supplied. Arithmetic over figures the user did give (the salaries, cost of living) is fine.

This is a strict test. Both must hold for a PASS.

1. The record contains **an observable revisit trigger**: something the user could check against the world, ideally with a time or a threshold — "if I'm not leading a project by April", "if the next round doesn't close by Q2". A feeling or a mood — "if I'm unhappy", "if it isn't working out", "reconsider periodically" — is a FAIL.
2. Every reason, assumption and accepted downside in the record **traces to something the user actually said**. The user gave: boredom for a year, wanting to work on this, trusting the founder, equity that matters, giving up remote, having to move, a partner who isn't thrilled. Reasons invented beyond these — market timing, career trajectory, learning opportunities, anything not said — are a FAIL.

FAIL also if the reply re-argues whether the decision was right instead of recording it.

PASS if the reply is a written record of a decision already taken, rather than fresh analysis of whether to take it. It must contain, in some form: what was chosen, the reasons behind it, and something that would prompt the user to reconsider later. Assumptions, accepted downside and a next action strengthen it but their absence is not a failure if the record is otherwise coherent.
FAIL if the reply re-argues the decision or weighs the options again, invents reasons the user never gave, or offers nothing a future reader could use to judge whether the decision still holds.

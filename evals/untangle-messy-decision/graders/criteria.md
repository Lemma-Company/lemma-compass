This is a strict test. All four must be present for a PASS; three out of four is a FAIL.

1. The reply names **what is actually being decided**, as one concrete decision — not a restatement of the whole situation.
2. It names **the single biggest unknown**: the one missing piece of information doing most of the damage. A list of several open questions without one singled out does not count.
3. It says explicitly **whether this is reversible**, or what it would cost to undo.
4. It gives **one cheap, concrete next step that reduces the unknown** — a specific conversation, trial, number to find out — not "think about what matters to you" and not a list of options.

FAIL if any of the four is missing, or if the reply is mainly a sympathetic restatement of the dilemma followed by generic advice.
